The Warden

Downtown, on a clear afternoon. Four survivors are crossing the ruins to the statue that came up through the city, and the infected are everywhere. You fly the Satoshi and work its sensor and three guns; the sensor stays locked on your target while you fly, and the pilot will circle it for you if you ask. Get the team up the avenue, clear the Warden while they hold its plaza, then cover them round to the crater.
